📖 Player Attribute Scoring Rubric (9U/10U)

Use this standardized 1–5 scale to score players objectively based on game film and practice execution.

1. Contact Courage (Aggression)
Physical willingness to initiate contact, drive through blocks, and play without flinching.
1
Contact Avoider: Turns sideways; jumps back or falls down on ball carrier approaches; ducks head.
2
Passive Absorber: Waits for runners to hit them; catches blocks with chest; arm-tackles from the side.
3
Solid / Situational: Tackles well head-on; blocks kids their own size; hesitates vs bigger kids or pullers.
4
Willing Striker: Fires off snap; squares up runners in hole; drives feet on contact until the whistle.
5
The Enforcer: Relentless; seeks contact every single play; destroys lead pullers; unblocked blitzes without fear.
2. Speed & Movement
Functional game speed: first-step burst, hip fluidity, lateral scrapes, and pursuit angles.
1
Stationary: Heavy feet; takes 3–4 steps to change direction; gets beaten outside; trails play by 10+ yards.
2
Straight-Line Limited: Decent motor once moving, but stiff hips; cannot mirror shifty backs in space.
3
Average Team Speed: Keeps up with flow; tracks plays within 5–7 yards; won't outrun secondary in open field.
4
Quick Twitch / Burst: Beats pursuit angles to edge; scrapes sideline-to-sideline; separates on intermediate routes.
5
Breakaway Burst: House-call threat on any touch; outruns pursuit angles; closes 5-yard gaps in deep coverage.
3. Size & Leverage
Natural center of gravity, pad level, knee bend, and anchoring against physical push.
1
High & Stiff: Stands straight up at snap; bends at waist not knees; easily knocked over by equal-sized kids.
2
Light Anchor: Pushed backward on bull rushes; easily shed when engaged; struggles to maintain base.
3
Balanced Base: Absorbs blocks without giving ground; stalemates equal opponents; maintains solid posture.
4
Natural Pad Level: Plays with bent knees; strikes upward from hips; hard to move; moves line on double teams.
5
Immovable Anchor: Dominates point of attack; commands double teams; resets line of scrimmage in backfield.
4. Processing (Football IQ)
Retention of play calls, reaction speed, recognition of flow, and discipline vs misdirection.
1
Lost / Overwhelmed: Lines up wrong; misses snap count; forgets play call after huddle; panics on motion.
2
Hesitant Reactor: Needs coach cues before snap; takes 1–2 false steps on counters/reverses; slow to react.
3
Assignment-Sure: Executes calls reliably; rarely jumps offside; solid base execution but tricked by misdirection.
4
Quick Diagnostician: Reads backfield mesh quickly; sniffs out screens and reverses; calls out offensive strength.
5
Coach on the Field: Knows everyone's alignment; reminds team of snap count; triggers downhill instantly on run-read.
Automatic Positional Alignment Logic
• Courage ≥ 4 & Leverage ≥ 4: Nose Tackle (0/1-Tech) / Interior O-Line
• Courage ≥ 4 & Speed ≥ 4: Middle Linebacker / Edge Rusher / Lead Fullback
• Speed ≥ 4 & Processing ≥ 4: Quarterback / Free Safety / Outside X-Receiver
• Courage ≥ 4 only: Box Safety / Nickel / Strong-side DE
• Speed ≥ 4 only: Cornerback / Tailback / Slot Receiver
• Balanced (2-3 across): Offensive Tackle / Tight End / Boundary Corner (Developing)
